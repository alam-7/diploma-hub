About DiplomaHub

"Understand Engineering. Learn Practically. Build the Future."

DiplomaHub is a free digital learning platform built specifically for MSBTE Diploma Engineering students. We bring together verified curriculum content, hands-on practical guides, a searchable equipment library, MCQ quizzes, previous year papers, and study resources — all in one place.

Learn

Access verified curriculum and study material for every branch and semester.

Practice

Hands-on practicals, lab manuals, and experiment guides for real skills.

Test

MCQ quizzes and previous year papers to evaluate and improve your knowledge.

Grow

Track your progress, earn XP, build streaks, and stay motivated.

🎓
